About the House

Over 25 years of craft, care, and colour.

At Vaffi Hair Salon & Aesthetics, hair is more than a service — it's an art form. For over two and a half decades we've welcomed Toronto into our chairs for expert colour, precision cutting, smoothing & straightening, luxury extensions, and complete aesthetics.

We listen first, so every cut, colour, and finish reflects the person in the mirror. From a quick blowout to a full transformation, you leave feeling seen, cared for, and wholly yourself.
